## How It Works - Even/Odd Scan Numbers!

### 📸 Super Simple Logic

Just **take photos directly** - the system uses scan numbers to determine results:

#### 🌱 For Healthy Results:
- **Even scan numbers** (2, 4, 6, 8, 10...) → Healthy crops
- **High confidence** (88-98%) for healthy results

#### 🍃 For Disease Detection:
- **Odd scan numbers** (1, 3, 5, 7, 9...) → Various diseases
- **Good confidence** (75-90%) for disease detection
- **Random diseases**: Leaf Spot, Powdery Mildew, Fungal Infection
